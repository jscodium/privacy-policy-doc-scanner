1. Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words with capitalized initial letters have specific meanings defined under the following conditions. These definitions have the same meaning whether they appear in singular or plural.

Definitions

  • Application or Service refers to DocScanner: PDF Scanner & OCR, the software program provided by us.
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Service.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data means data collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (for example, ad impressions and crash logs).
  • Local Data refers to scans, images, PDF files, extracted text, signatures, settings, and any other files created or imported within the App and stored on your device's storage.

2. Collecting and Using Your Data

Local Data Processing

The App works as an on-device utility. All document scanning, image enhancement, OCR (image to text and PDF to text), PDF creation and editing, background removal, and signature creation take place locally on your device. Your documents and personal content do not leave your device and are not collected by us.

The App provides the following features, all processed on your device:

  • Document and ID Scanning: Scan paper documents, receipts, notes, business cards, ID cards, passports, driving licenses, and bank cards using the camera or by importing images from your gallery. Scans are enhanced, cropped, and filtered locally.
  • OCR (Image to Text and PDF to Text): Text is extracted from images and PDF pages on your device. OCR works offline.
  • PDF Tools: Image to PDF, Text to PDF, Merge PDF, Split PDF, Edit PDF, PDF to Image, PDF to Long Image, and Print PDF are all performed locally.
  • Lock and Unlock PDF: Passwords you enter to lock or unlock a PDF are used only on your device to perform the operation. We do not collect, store, or transmit your passwords.
  • Image Background Remover: Backgrounds are removed from your images on your device.
  • Signature Maker: Signatures you draw are created and saved on your device only.

No Server Storage

We do not collect, receive, or store any of your documents, scanned images, ID card or passport scans, PDF files, extracted text, or signatures on external servers.

Printing

When you print a PDF or image, the App uses Android's built-in print framework. Your file is sent only to the printer or print service that you choose.

3. App Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide its core features, the App requests specific device permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

1. Camera Access

  • Purpose: Required to capture documents, ID cards, and other items for scanning, Image to PDF, OCR, and PDF creation and editing. The camera is an optional hardware feature, so the App can still be used on devices without a camera by importing images from the gallery.
  • Privacy: The camera feed and captured images are processed on your device. No photos or videos are secretly taken, saved, or transmitted to any server.

2. Device Storage

  • Android 12L (API 32) and lower: The App may request read and write storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) so that it can open your images and PDF files and save the files you create (images, PDFs, text files) on your device.
  • Android 13 and higher: The App does not require broad storage permissions. It uses Android's secure system pickers instead:
    • Importing images and PDFs: The App only accesses the exact files you select.
    • Saving and exporting files: When you export or save a file, Android's system file chooser lets you choose where to save it.

3. Network and WiFi State

  • Purpose: Required to display advertisements, check for app updates, load remote configuration settings, send crash reports and diagnostic information, and check network connectivity. Your documents are not uploaded using this connection.

4. Vibration

  • Purpose: Provides subtle haptic feedback during certain actions in the App.

5. Advertising ID

  • Purpose: Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to deliver advertisements and measure ad performance in compliance with Google Play Policies.

4. User Consent for Permissions

Sensitive permissions, such as Camera access, require your explicit consent before they are activated. You can deny permission requests. However, denying Camera access will prevent live scanning from working. Importing images from your gallery and using the PDF tools will remain available.
